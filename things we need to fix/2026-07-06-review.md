Reviewed: uncommitted diff (season-pass/free-games feature, `useGameFrameScale`

## 1. Free-games counter bugs across unrelated match modes (HIGH — fix before merging)

`app/api/match/[matchId]/route.ts:702-729` increments `free-games:<addr>` on
**every** completed match (any mode), via the unconditional `incrementFreeGame`
call inside the `if (matchEndSnapshot)` block.

But the thing free games are supposed to gate is narrower:
- `app/api/season-pass/enter/route.ts:77-79` only grants sponsored entry for
  `mode === "ranked"`.
- The UI only shows/enforces the free-games allowance for `ranked` and
  `vshouse` match types (`app/create/page.tsx:264-268, 473-503`).
- But `vshouse` is sent to the server as `mode: "wager"`
  (`app/select-character/page.tsx:51`) — identical to a real money 1v1 wager
  match. There is no server-side field that distinguishes "free vs-house bot
  match" from "real paid wager match against another human."

Net effect: a player who only plays real-money wager matches (never touching
ranked/vshouse) will still burn down their 2 free credits, because every
completed wager match increments the same counter. The first time they try a
genuinely free ranked or vs-house match, they get bounced to "buy a season
pass" despite never having used a free credit for its intended purpose.

**Fix:**
- Only call `incrementFreeGame` when `m.mode === "ranked"`.
- Add a real server-side signal to distinguish vshouse bot matches from paid
  wager matches (e.g. a `vsBot: boolean` on `ServerMatch`), then gate the
  vshouse free-game consumption on that instead of `mode === "wager"`.

## 2. Payout signature bypass is spoofable (LOW — not fund-theft, but worth tightening)

`app/api/payout/route.ts:106-117`: when the client sends `isMiniPay: true`,
signature verification is skipped entirely and the only check is
"claimant address == match.winnerAddress." Both `isMiniPay` and `address` are
client-supplied, and both `matchId` and the winner's address are readable via
the match GET endpoint. So anyone who knows a matchId can call this route
claiming `isMiniPay: true` and trigger the payout without ever holding that
wallet.

This is **not** a theft vector — funds always go to `match.winnerAddress`,
never to the caller — but it means the signature check provides no real
authorization for MiniPay-flagged requests, and a third party can force the
payout tx (and, for the G$ path, an early Superfluid stream start) at will.

**Fix (optional, low priority):** if you want the MiniPay path to mean
anything, verify MiniPay-ness some other way (e.g. server-side check against
known MiniPay session data) rather than trusting a client-supplied boolean.
